04 / THE DIASPORA DESK

\n

Buying from afar.
Never in the dark.

\n

See the home over video, know what needs to be checked and stay in the loop through each step.

\n
Can I view a property remotely?

Ask for a video walkthrough. We’ll help you prepare questions about the home, development, and immediate neighbourhood.

\n
What is on the document checklist?

Start with the seller’s authority to sell, available title and survey documents, and the written terms. The exact checks depend on the property.

\n
Can someone represent me in Lagos?

Tell us what you need to handle remotely. We’ll help you identify the steps to discuss with your lawyer, including whether a power of attorney is appropriate.

\n
What if my budget doesn’t match?

We’ll be upfront and explain tradeoffs in location, property type and stage of completion, so you can decide what to change.
